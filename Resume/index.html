Summary

Cell therapy scientist with 15 years in biotech and academic core labs, taking CAR-T and NK-cell programs from discovery through IND. Focused on donor-driven CAR-T biology, pairing functional assays, single-cell cytokine profiling and in vivo work with R-based analysis and dashboards. Co-inventor on two granted US patents.
